Bring your own library: Leaflet

The same map three ways (the levels of the third-party guide). / 같은 지도를 세 가지 수준으로 가져옵니다(서드파티 통합 가이드의 수준).

L0 — direct use in one component / 한 곳에서 직접 사용

vf.vfunc with onMount / onDestroy creates and removes the Leaflet map. See l0.js.

L1 — an app wrapper / 앱 전용 래퍼

A factory of your app, reused on several screens, with the props your app needs. See l1.js.

L2 — a contract adapter / 계약 어댑터

vfMapLeaflet follows the base contract (lib, .instance, refresh, destroy, { sender, event, data }), so it passes the contract tests and could be contributed. See vfunc-map-leaflet.js.
